## Personal Data Collected and Purpose of Processing

We process personal data only to the extent necessary to operate the platform and conduct the hackathon.

### Registration and Participation

During registration, we collect the following data:

- First and last name
- Email address
- University / training institution (optional)
- Study program (optional)
- Semester (optional)
- LinkedIn profile URL (optional)
- Category preference
- Dietary restrictions and allergies (for catering)

**Legal basis:** Performance of contract (event organization, Art. 6 FADP) and legitimate interests (security, communication).

### Authentication

For login and access security, we use:

- Password (stored encrypted using bcrypt)
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) via email code
- Session cookies (httpOnly, technically necessary)

**Legal basis:** Legitimate interest (platform security).

### Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter, we store your email address to send you event-related information.

**Legal basis:** Consent (Art. 6 para. 6 FADP). Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

### Uploaded Files and GitHub Repositories

As part of team collaboration, participants may store files and repository links on the platform. This data is used exclusively for the purpose of running the hackathon.

**Legal basis:** Performance of contract.

### Web Push Notifications

During the event, we optionally offer browser push notifications. If you enable them, we store an encrypted browser subscription token (PushSubscription) and the associated endpoint of your browser. This data is used exclusively to send event notifications (e.g., schedule updates, announcements).

- The subscription can be withdrawn at any time in your browser settings.
- Push subscriptions are deleted immediately after the event ends.

**Legal basis:** Consent (explicit browser permission, Art. 6 para. 6 FADP).

### Server Logs

When accessing our website, technical access data (IP address, browser type, timestamp) is stored by our hosting provider. This data is not linked to personal data.

**Legal basis:** Legitimate interest (operation and security).

## Data Security

We protect your personal data through the following technical and organizational measures:

- Encrypted transmission via HTTPS/TLS
- Passwords stored as bcrypt hashes (cost factor 12)
- 2FA codes stored as hashes in the database
- Authentication tokens in httpOnly cookies (XSS protection)
- Two-factor authentication for administrative access
- Access restrictions based on the principle of least privilege

## Your Rights

Under the Swiss FADP, you have the following rights:

- **Right of access (Art. 25 FADP):** You may request information about the personal data stored about you at any time.
- **Right to rectification (Art. 32 FADP):** You may request the correction of inaccurate data.
- **Right to erasure:** You may request the deletion of your data, unless legal retention obligations apply. Account deletion is available directly on the platform under "Profile Settings."
- **Right to data portability (Art. 28 FADP):** You may request that your data be provided in a commonly used format.
- **Right to object:** You may object to data processing based on legitimate interests.
- **Withdrawal of consent:** Consent given (e.g., for the newsletter) may be withdrawn at any time.

To exercise your rights, please contact: [{{email-contact-hslu}}]({{email-url-contact-hslu}})

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC): [www.edoeb.admin.ch](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch)
